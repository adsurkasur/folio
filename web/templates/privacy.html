Privacy Policy

arinahub · Effective: September 2026

Welcome to Folio. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you understand how your information is handled.

1. Information We Collect

Folio is designed to be a minimalist, anonymous publishing platform. We collect the minimum amount of information necessary to run the service:

  • Cookies: We use a single functional cookie (folio_author_token) to identify you as the author of the articles you publish. This allows you to edit or delete your content later. No tracking or marketing cookies are used.
  • Uploaded Content: Any text or images you publish are stored on our servers to be served publicly.
  • Log Data: We temporarily log IP addresses for rate-limiting and anti-spam purposes to protect our infrastructure.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information exclusively to provide, maintain, and improve the Folio service. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

3. Data Retention and Deletion

Articles and images are kept indefinitely until deleted. You can delete your articles at any time using the "DELETE" button on the article page, provided you still have the author cookie on your device. Once deleted, the content is permanently removed from our active database.

4. Third-Party Links

Articles published by users may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those external sites.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
